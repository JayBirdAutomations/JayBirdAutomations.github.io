About Jaybird Automations | Las Vegas AI Agency

Jaybird Automations is a Las Vegas, Nevada AI agency building practical, revenue-generating automation systems for local businesses — chatbots, AI agents, N8N workflows, custom AI software, and broadcast-quality AI commercials.

Who we are

Jaybird Automations was founded in 2024 by Jay, an AI engineer who got tired of watching Las Vegas small businesses pay national agencies thousands per month for cookie-cutter "AI strategy" that never shipped anything. The thesis was simple: build real systems, deploy them fast, charge fair prices, and prove every tool on our own business before selling it to anyone else.

We are a solo-led, AI-native agency — not a web design shop that added "AI" to the menu in 2024. Every system we deliver runs in production for Jaybird first. Our own lead generation engine scraped and scored 650+ Las Vegas businesses across 10 industries. Our chatbot answers questions on this website 24/7. Our blog is generated by an autonomous N8N + Claude workflow. If a tool doesn't work for us, we don't sell it.

Today Jaybird Automations serves law firms, dental and medical practices, contractors, auto repair shops, restaurants, real estate agencies, and professional service companies across Las Vegas, Henderson, North Las Vegas, Summerlin, and the broader Nevada market.

Why Las Vegas

National marketing agencies treat Las Vegas as one segment of a national rollout — copy-paste strategies that ignore how this market actually works. We don't. We work exclusively with Southern Nevada businesses and we know this market: the seasonality, the competitive density in industries like personal injury law and HVAC, the way Strip-adjacent hospitality operates differently from Summerlin professional services, the local Google search patterns that drive real foot traffic versus tourist clicks.

That focus shows up in every system we build. Your chatbot doesn't just answer questions — it qualifies leads using the actual buying signals Las Vegas customers send. Your automation doesn't just connect apps — it respects local response time expectations. Your AI commercial doesn't just look polished — it speaks to a Vegas audience.
